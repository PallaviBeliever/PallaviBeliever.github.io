About

More than a résumé.

My name is a little love story — Pallavi chosen by my mother, Priyanka by my father. I carry both, always.

I'm a Finance & Analytics professional with five years of experience turning complex data into decisions that matter — currently at Goldman Sachs, where I work at the intersection of technology, compliance, and process transformation.

Outside the office, I shoot streets, skies, and the in-between moments that most people walk past. Photography taught me to slow down — and that same instinct led me to found Dheemi Gati, a social cause campaign I started during my MBA to advocate for road safety and the lives lost to preventable accidents.

I believe in rigorous thinking, beautiful craft, and work that creates real change — whether that's in a spreadsheet, a photograph, or a movement.
